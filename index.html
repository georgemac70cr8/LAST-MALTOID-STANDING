Rules

“Last Twat Standing” is a Premier League elimination game.

  • Without initial payment of £20, a player will be eliminated before the round has started, even if they have chosen a team. See the DEPOSIT £ button for the deposit details.
  • A round starts on the opening day when everyone selects a team and ends when nobody is left in, either from having the one person winning the final selection or if all players have been eliminated for not choosing a winning team.
  • A new round starts directly after the previous round ends, bringing all players back into the game with a brand new list of 20 teams to choose from and a new payment of £20 to be paid.
  • Each week, pick one Premier League team to win.
  • If that team wins, you survive to play in the next game week.
  • If it draws or loses, you’re eliminated.
  • You can’t pick the same team more than once per round.
  • Picks must be submitted before the stated deadline.
  • If you miss the deadline, you’ll be assigned a default pick, namely the first team available to you alphabetically (AFC Bournemouth, Arsenal, Aston Villa, etc...).
  • ONLY the last remaining player wins. There is NO splitting the prize pool between players. A person either wins the round or is eliminated during the round or is entered back in during a rollover.
  • If everyone remaining loses in the same game week, all are eliminated and a rollover occurs.
  • With a rollover, every player must pay a further £20 so the prize is boosted.
  • A player can choose not to take part in the rollover if they do not want to pay extra. However, they do not get a refund for what they have already paid in.
  • Cup matches, postponed fixtures, and abandoned games usually have organiser-specific handling.

Example: Week 1 you pick Arsenal and they win; Week 2 you must select another team, such as Liverpool. HOWEVER, if everybody picks Arsenal in week 1 and they lose, everybody is eliminated and a rollover occurs.
